ACID Guarantees in SyncCore Simulation

Atomicity

All booking and payment steps execute completely or rollback entirely. No orphan tickets or uncharged bookings.

Consistency

Foreign keys, unique constraints on `uk_booking_show_seat`, and triggers preserve seat invariant states.

Isolation

Pessimistic row locking (`SELECT FOR UPDATE`) prevents dirty reads and double booking in high contention.

Durability

InnoDB write-ahead redo logs guarantee committed ticket bookings persist across system crashes.
